join in either direction.

hand" precisely because an equality test compiles untouched against a new variant, and `IdleTimeout`
arriving during Phase 31 is proof the variant set grows. This repo consistently prefers structural
guards over hand audits — `decide_action`'s wildcard-free match, `ParsedCapture` making dropped lines
representable — and D-06 continues that line.

the time the classifier runs the derived status genuinely is `Success` and this fix passes cleanly
over the exploit. Criterion 3 and criterion 4 are separate deliverables; that separation is itself
part of the deliverable and is recorded in the rewritten doc comment.

Output: the rewritten match, a 42-cell sweep exercising `layer0` in both states, four named controls
(NC-1 through NC-4), and the downstream half of criterion 4's demonstration.
