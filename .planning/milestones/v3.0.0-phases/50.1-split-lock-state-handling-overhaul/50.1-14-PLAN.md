three remaining stale-answer paths — resume after a killed waiter (D-06), the Supervise re-scan carry-over (D-07) and

and consume it through one first-wins claim, so `devflow ship` and a live waiter can never both finalize one approval
(today only the long per-phase lock keeps them apart, `pipeline_gate.rs` ~L556-559; that lock stays until 50.1-20). Seven
files: the waiter, its callers' cleanups and the tests that pinned the old slot all change in one step, or the suite is
red between plans; the edits outside `pipeline_gate.rs`/`pipeline_launch.rs` are a `cleanup` → `close` substitution.
Output: the switched waiter, `ship_override` claim, `close` everywhere, and the V-15 (CLI), V-17, V-20, V-21, V-22 tests.
