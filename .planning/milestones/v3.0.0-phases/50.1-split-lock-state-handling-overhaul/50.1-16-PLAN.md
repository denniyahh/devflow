<objective>
Make `start` and `resume` record their supervisor before it exists (D-01): reserve at the top, check-and-reserve in one
step under the write lock, hand the reservation to the spawn, promote it at the publish — and drop the long per-phase
lock from both, since the reservation now excludes a second launcher (D-08).

Purpose: Phase 50's crash-table row A7 (T-50-90) — a launcher that dies during its publish wait leaves a live supervisor
nothing records, and a second launch is not refused. With a pending record whose liveness lock the supervisor inherited,
the guard sees it from the moment it is reserved. S(N)→N+1 (row C4) is 50.1-17; `stop`/`status` over a pending
supervisor is 50.1-18; D-17's per-phase holder, which `start`/`resume` claim just before this plan's reservation so
`stop` can reach them before their spawn and a refused launcher writes nothing, is 50.1-29. This plan runs after 50.1-15 because both edit the CLI's
`commands.rs`/`pipeline_launch.rs` (15 may have to migrate a CLI test that still names a deleted gate function).
Output: `reserve_launch`, the pending-aware guard, the reservation hand-off and promotion, the fixtures' test holds, and
the V-01, V-03 (CLI), V-04, V-05 tests; `start_lock_e2e`'s lock-premised tests rewritten.
</objective>
