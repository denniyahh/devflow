<objective>
Make the six fixed-budget polling helpers in `crates/devflow-cli/tests/phase7_cli.rs` use one configurable budget,
and make a timeout name the budget it used (999.55; requirement TEST-02).

Today `wait_for` and `wait_for_pid` poll `200 × 25ms` = 5s, and `wait_for_state_cleared`, `wait_for_stopped`,
`wait_for_settled` and `wait_for_gate` poll `400 × 25ms` = 10s. Each has its own loop and bound. All six fail under
load: on 2026-07-28 at the 5s budget, and on 2026-09-19 at the 10s budget, both in the devcontainer job or the local
container gate while sibling jobs competed for the runner.

Purpose: a slow runner can be given more time without editing the helpers, and a timeout says which budget failed.
This plan does NOT show the flake is gone. Only CI run history after the merge could show that.

Output: the edited test file, the devcontainer workflow, and the setup checklist.
</objective>

  <decision>
    How many seconds the phase7_cli wait budget gives each wait when `CI` is set: the value of `CI_WAIT_SECS`.
  </decision>
  <context>
    Facts that bear on the choice:
    - A wait returns as soon as its condition holds. A larger budget costs nothing on a passing run. It only delays
      the report when something is really broken.
    - The recorded failures happened at the 5s budget (2026-07-28) and at the 10s budget (2026-09-19). Both were in
      the devcontainer job or the local container gate, while sibling jobs competed for the runner.
    - Each child DevFlow process these tests spawn gets a gate timeout of 60 seconds (`run_devflow_inner`). A wait at
      or above that length can outlast the gate it is waiting on. The child then times out underneath the test, and
      the failure no longer names the budget.
    - The 999.55 backlog entry's fix direction suggested about 30 seconds under CI.
    - The devcontainer job has a 40-minute timeout. The suite normally runs in under two seconds.
  </context>
  <options>
    <option id="ci-60">
      <name>60 seconds</name>
      <pros>Six times today's longest budget. The most headroom against runner contention.</pros>
      <cons>Equal to the child gate timeout. One slow wait can use up the child's whole gate window, so the timeout fails in a way that no longer names the budget.</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="ci-15">
      <name>15 seconds</name>
      <pros>A genuine hang is reported fast. Well inside the child gate timeout.</pros>
      <cons>Only 1.5 times the 10s budget that already failed under contention on 2026-09-19.</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="ci-30">
      <name>30 seconds</name>
      <pros>Three times the longest budget that failed. Half the child gate timeout. Matches the backlog entry's fix direction.</pros>
      <cons>A genuine hang takes up to 30 seconds per wait to report.</cons>
    </option>
    <option id="ci-120">
      <name>120 seconds</name>
      <pros>Rules out contention as a cause for almost any runner.</pros>
      <cons>Twice the child gate timeout, so a slow wait can outlast the gate it waits on. A regression that stalls many waits could run the job toward its timeout.</cons>
    </option>
  </options>
  <resume-signal>Name one option id: ci-60, ci-15, ci-30 or ci-120. Give the reason for the choice.</resume-signal>
