## Rejected after checking

- **Stop at a gate aborts and deletes the state** (agy-adversarial 1): predates Phase 50 and is step 1 of
  OPERATIONS.md's `stop` row; the "stays resumable" sentence cited is about a signal sent to a supervisor.
- **Backstop refuses after the leader dies during the SIGTERM grace** (agy-adversarial 5): the documented residual
  limit — members without a proven leader are never signalled.
- **Forged state, sidecar or listener attacks** (agy-security 1, 2, 5, 7; pi-security 1, 6): each needs write access,
  as the same user, to the owner-only (0700) socket directory or to the project's state; that user can already
  signal those processes. The hostile-repository variant fails: git cannot carry a socket, and launch ids are random.
- **Self-signal** (agy-security 6): `safe_target` excludes the caller's group; a group whose id equals the caller's
  pid is the caller's own group.
- **A stop after the last safe point is lost** (pi-adversarial 3): the run has already ended, or 50-23 forwards the
  stop to the successor.
- **Environment leakage to agents** (agy-security 8, pi-security 7): predates Phase 50, outside its scope.
