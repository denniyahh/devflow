<objective>
Make the in-process advance safe to stop and safe to hand off (R-1, R-2). The supervisor already answers `shutdown`
throughout its advance (50-07, 50-11); this plan makes the advance honour a stop at defined safe points, makes the gate
wait cancellable, and fixes the handoff order so the state's handle never points at nothing.

Decision recorded (Claude remit, RESEARCH Finding 3 preference): the stop token reaches the safe points through a
`thread_local!` RAII guard engaged by `run_monitor` — not a process-global static (parallel in-crate tests would share
it) and not a parameter threaded through `launch_stage`'s callers.

Purpose: `devflow stop` (50-14) can end a run at any moment without a lost state write, a leaked lock, or a next stage
launching behind it.
Output: safe points, hand-off marking and test hold points in `pipeline_launch.rs`; `Gates::poll_response_cancellable`;
`state.supervisor` cleared where `monitor_pid` is cleared at a `--until` stop.
</objective>

lock — the publish — and stores N+1's record as its successor; 5) `advance_with` returns, the lock drops, N marks

which is right for a socket caller (it retries against N+1) and wrong for a signal (no caller retries), so N enters
`HandedOff` only after the advance has returned, keeping every stop that lands after safe point 3 a flagged
`Advancing` stop; 50-23 forwards such a stop to the successor. Safe points
