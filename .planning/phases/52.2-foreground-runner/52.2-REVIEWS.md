### [CRITICAL] CI `service` job in Plan 52.2-03 relies on unverified assumptions about non-login `systemd --user` availability in GitHub Actions
- Where: `.planning/phases/52.2-foreground-runner/52.2-03-PLAN.md:25, 112-124` (`then sudo loginctl enable-linger "$USER", export XDG_RUNTIME_DIR=/run/user/$(id -u), wait bounded for systemctl --user show -p Version --value to succeed, then scripts/check.sh service`) and `.planning/phases/52.2-foreground-runner/52.2-RESEARCH.md:330` (Assumptions Log A2).
