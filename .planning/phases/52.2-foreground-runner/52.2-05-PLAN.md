On FlagForSafePoint (Advancing or GateWait) the flag alone is the effect: the gate poll and the next safe point read it. It is the only reader of the signal pipe. run_runner starts it right after install_signal_pipe and before notify_ready, and keeps the SignalDispatcher alive until it returns. Plans 52.2-12 and 52.2-24 move it with the kept stop primitives and must not delete it with `supervisor/`.

The spike recorded nothing without a supervisor record, so this recording path is new code.
