## Accomplishments
- `run_service` in `scripts/check.sh` (not in `all`): manager probe, cargo-nextest requirement, `cargo nextest run -p devflow --test service_e2e --run-ignored only --no-tests=fail`. On the host it ran 2 service_e2e tests, both passed.
- The pre-push hook runs `scripts/check.sh service` on the host after the container gate (both branches).
- `ci.yml` gains a `service` job, last, on `ubuntu-24.04` with no `container:`, `shell: bash`, `timeout-minutes: 30`, `permissions: contents: read`, cargo-nextest 0.9.152 installed through a multi-line `run: |`. Its negative-control step unsets `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` and `DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS` and requires the target to fail with "no systemd user manager answers". That control is a **proxy for an unreachable manager**: it leaves `systemctl --user` nothing to connect through, it does not remove a manager. One `run:` step then enables lingering, exports `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, waits up to 60 s (starting `user@UID.service` after 10 s), exports `DBUS_SESSION_BUS_ADDRESS` when the bus socket exists, and runs the target. The header comment names `service` as the one job outside the pinned image.
- Parity guards: `LIVE_CI_JOB_KEYS` is `[&str; 6]` with `service` last, the "six" wording, and `scripts/check.sh service` in the delegation targets. Against the old ci.yml, `job_splitter_finds_exactly_the_live_ci_jobs` and `ci_workflow_delegates_to_the_shared_check_script` both fail with their intended messages.
- The pre-push fixture commits a passing `scripts/check.sh` stub (without it, 3 success-expecting tests failed with exit 127 on the missing script). `a_failing_service_target_refuses_the_push` fails against the old hook ("status Some(0)") and passes against the new one.
