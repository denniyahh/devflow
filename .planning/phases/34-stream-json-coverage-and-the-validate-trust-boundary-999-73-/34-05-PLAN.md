delivery floor — at least one stage newly widened on a newly captured run, or an explicit escalation
saying why none could be.

Purpose: this is the only part of the phase that needs a live agent run, and the only part that
trips CLAUDE.md's "never run git operations while an executor holds the working tree" rule. It is
also the part with the most ways to produce a confident-looking non-result: a capture taken by a
binary that never contained the widened constant, a `__monitor` smoke run offered as production
evidence, or a doc-comment "reason" that is an omission with a sentence around it.

**Why this plan has six task elements rather than the usual two or three:** D-02 Amendment 1 makes
the evidence gate **commit-time, not build-time** — widen in the working tree, capture, and let the
evidence decide what gets committed. Widening, capturing and deciding what to commit are therefore
one indivisible unit; splitting them across plans would mean one plan commits a widening a later
plan might have to revert, which is exactly what D-02 forbids.

Output: the widened constant with per-stage evidence entries and per-stage recorded reasons, the
committed and PII-scrubbed per-stage captures, the drain analysis criterion 2 requires, the resolved
answer to RESEARCH.md's Open Question 1, and the backlog entries D-12 and D-04 oblige.
