A missing **report** is distinct from a failed **reader**. 8. **“Exactly once” lacks a durable scope and arbitration rule.**  
   `.planning/phases/52-subtract-and-stabilize/52-CONTEXT.md:117–118` promises one automatic relaunch followed by an open gate on another denial.
