<closure_threshold_and_its_justification>
**Threshold: 11 observations at one settled head SHA — 6 of the push-gate shape and 5 of
CI-on-branch. All 11 must be green, consecutive, and on the same tree.**

The asymmetry has to be stated before the number is: **one reproduction falsifies "closed";
proving closure is proving a negative and can only ever bound a residual.**
`25-CI-OBSERVATION.md` §"Limits of this evidence" reasons this out for the positive case
("This is a positive reproduction, so it does not need the five-trial argument"). This plan
is the negative case, so it needs the argument, and it needs to be honest about what the
argument buys.

**Part 1 — five consecutive green `scripts/check-in-container.sh all` runs at the pushed SHA,
plus the `pre-push` hook's own run of the identical command, for six.**

This is the only shape that has ever reproduced the defect: 2 failures in 2 attempts, versus
0 in 17 across every warm shape. From that 2-of-2 observation, the exact-binomial 95%
one-sided lower bound on its per-run reproduction probability is `p >= 0.05^(1/2) = 0.224`.
Six greens bound the residual at `0.776^6 ~= 0.218` against that pessimistic bound, and at
`0.5^6 ~= 0.016` against the ~50% per-run rate `ROADMAP.md` records for 999.47 ("cost two
retries on 2026-07-27 alone"). Neither is zero, and the pessimistic figure is frankly weak on
its own — which is exactly why this part does not stand alone.

**Part 2 — five consecutive COMPLETED green CI `Test`-job runs on that same head SHA.**

The count `5` is inherited from 25-10's own trial design, not invented here, so this plan's
evidence is not weaker than the plan it supersedes. Its derivation there: `0.5^5 = 1/32 ~=
3.1%` residual against the recorded ~50% rate. Its purpose here is different from Part 1's:
19-RESEARCH.md **D-11** — *"Verify on a branch with CI. Local-green is explicitly
insufficient"* — is this project's standing verification standard for this class of race, and
a standard is satisfied by observing CI, not by a probability. `25-VALIDATION.md`'s own 25e
exception restates it: *"can therefore only be confirmed by CI-on-branch stability across
several pushes."* Part 2 discharges the standard. Part 1 does the statistical work, because
Part 1 is the sensitive instrument.

**Combined.** Treating the two shapes as exchangeable — generous to the defect, since CI's
`Test` job is the *less* sensitive of the two — 11 greens bound the residual at
`0.776^11 ~= 0.061` against the pessimistic bound and `0.5^11 ~= 0.0005` against the recorded
rate.

**What the record is therefore allowed to say.** `no reproduction across 11 observations at
<sha>`, with the residual stated. Not more. The word that means closed belongs to the human
in Task 3, and even then the real load is carried by the **structural** argument — 25-11's
measured census plus a bounded exec-visibility barrier at every vulnerable site, and 25-12's
age floor in the production reaper — with the observations acting as corroboration that the
structure behaves as designed. That ordering (structure argues, observation corroborates) is
lifted verbatim from 25-10's own `## Limits of this evidence` requirement, and it is the
ordering the phase got wrong the first time.

**A streak may not span a tree change.** Any commit between observation *k* and *k+1* voids
the streak; it restarts from zero at the new SHA. This is why Task 1 Step 0's bookkeeping
commits land BEFORE the first observation and why no further push may occur until Task 2's
CI streak completes.
</closure_threshold_and_its_justification>
