<objective>
Close CR-01, the one failed truth in 47-VERIFICATION.md, and add the operator's wording mitigation for
review finding 4.

**CR-01.** The delivered policy (CODE_STAGE_POLICY) and the resume instruction
(checkpoint_auto_decide_prompt) both require decision reasoning in the final message.
COMPLETION_PROTOCOL, which both prompts end with, says the whole final message must be the
DEVFLOW_RESULT line and nothing else. No final message can satisfy both.

**Fix, per operator decision 2026-09-11 (A1):** a wording change only. Reasoning goes ABOVE the
DEVFLOW_RESULT line. That line stays the last line of the final message, with nothing after it. No
DEVFLOW_DECISION record, no new marker, no parser change. This follows D-04's single-definition
pattern: the rule is stated once, in COMPLETION_PROTOCOL, and never re-stated in the policy or the
resume prompt.

**Finding 4, per operator decision 2026-09-12.** A resumed agent that restates the checkpoint's
gate-declaration line in its reasoning could make a *failed* resume read as a new checkpoint. The
mitigation is one sentence in checkpoint_auto_decide_prompt ONLY, telling the agent to name the gate
in prose and not copy that line. A test pins that the sentence does not itself carry the detector's
match shape. A prompt cannot enforce what the model writes; T-47-19 records that residual.

Purpose: DECN-03's audit trail becomes producible without breaking the result protocol. DECN-02's
FullExecute policy, which also demands reasoning, gets the same fix on all six adapters, because
every one of them ends with the same constant.

Output: one reworded constant, one added resume sentence, two contract tests with controls, a
corrected ARCHITECTURE.md sentence, and nine re-blessed baselines, each byte-identical to its base
with exactly the expected substitutions.

**Why the parser needs no change:** `parse_marker_lines` (agent_result.rs:2016-2044) walks lines from
the bottom within a 4000-character budget and takes the first `DEVFLOW_RESULT:` line whose JSON
parses. Reasoning above the line is already accepted. The new test binds that property to the
prompt's own text at the production boundary `evaluate_layer1`, and pins where the budget, not the
prompt, decides.

  `turn_two_resume_prompt.snap` also carries the resume paragraph as one line (checked at b3b47ea).

  the constant, or call `checkpoint_auto_decide_prompt`, so they stay green (29 non-snapshot
  `prompt::tests::` passed after both edits at planning time).
- **Literal copies:** no copy of the protocol or resume text exists outside prompt.rs and the
  snapshots in tracked files outside `.planning/`. One near-copy is inside prompt.rs:
  `VALIDATE_VERDICT_CONTRACT` (:314-315) ends with the same two source lines as COMPLETION_PROTOCOL
  (:54-55). It is out of scope, and a search-and-replace would hit it.
