<objective>
Close CR-01, the one failed truth in 47-VERIFICATION.md. The delivered policy (CODE_STAGE_POLICY) and
the resume instruction (checkpoint_auto_decide_prompt) both require decision reasoning in the final
message. COMPLETION_PROTOCOL, which both prompts end with, says the whole final message must be the
DEVFLOW_RESULT line and nothing else. No final message can satisfy both.

**Fix, per operator decision 2026-09-11 (A1):** a wording change only. Reasoning goes ABOVE the
DEVFLOW_RESULT line. That line stays the last line of the final message, with nothing after it. No
DEVFLOW_DECISION record, no new marker, no parser change. This follows D-04's single-definition
pattern: the rule is stated once, in COMPLETION_PROTOCOL, and never re-stated in the policy or the
resume prompt.

Purpose: DECN-03's audit trail becomes producible without breaking the result protocol. DECN-02's
FullExecute policy, which also demands reasoning, gets the same fix on all six adapters, because
every one of them ends with the same constant.

Output: one reworded constant, one prompt-to-parser contract test with a paired control, a corrected
ARCHITECTURE.md sentence, and nine re-blessed baselines from a diff confined to exactly two lines each.

**Why the parser needs no change:** `parse_marker_lines` (agent_result.rs:2016-2044) walks lines from
the bottom within a 4000-character budget and takes the first `DEVFLOW_RESULT:` line whose JSON
parses. Reasoning above the line is already accepted; existing tests
`parse_marker_lines_returns_last_marker_in_long_output` and `parse_marker_only_in_last_4000_chars`
prove both budget directions. The new test binds that property to the prompt's own text, at the
production boundary `evaluate_layer1`.

**Blast radius, stated in full.** COMPLETION_PROTOCOL ends every stage prompt on every adapter.
- **Snapshots:** all nine committed snapshots contain both of the protocol lines being changed, each
  exactly once (checked against the b3b47ea tree at planning time).
- **Existing tests:** those use `ends_with(COMPLETION_PROTOCOL)` / `contains(COMPLETION_PROTOCOL)` on
  the constant, so they stay green.
- **Literal copies of the text:** a planning-time search found none outside prompt.rs and the
  snapshots (tests, docs/, README.md, OPERATIONS.md).
- **agents/mod.rs:** its prompt-count tests check `contains("DEVFLOW_RESULT")`, which is unaffected.

**Deliberately not changed:** the inline instructions in `ship_stage_prompt`, `idempotent_stage_prompt`,
`workflow_plan_prompt` and `workflow_ship_prompt`, and `VALIDATE_VERDICT_CONTRACT`. None of those
instructions asks for anything else in the same message, so nothing contradicts them. Changing them
would widen the diff into prompts this gap does not involve.
</objective>
