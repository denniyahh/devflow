<objective>
Extend D-01's record-before-spawn to the stage handoff: stage N's supervisor reserves and records N+1 as pending
before spawning it, so a crash between N+1's spawn and its promotion (crash-table row C4) leaves a state that names
N+1, and give every new window this opens a defined end.

Purpose: 50.1-16 covered launches from `start`/`resume`. A handoff is the other launch path; without a pending record a
dead S(N) leaves N+1 unrecorded and live (T-50-91). The `doctor` classifier must also stop calling that pending N+1 an
orphan (Pitfall 7).
Output: the handoff pending save, the clearing paths, the V-02 test and the window tests, the classifier fix.
</objective>
