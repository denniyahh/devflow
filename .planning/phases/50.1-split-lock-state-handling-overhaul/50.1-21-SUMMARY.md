# Phase 50.1 Plan 21: Recover decides liveness without a lock Summary

**`recover --clean` now decides on a fresh read under the state write lock from the supervisor sockets, the reservation, the gate registrations and the D-17 holder, so it no longer deletes a live supervisor's run (999.166 item 2), and the per-phase pid-lock API is deleted from `devflow-core`.**

## What argues against this result

- **A STALE socket the backstop has proved gone now refuses at first.** `refuse_a_live_supervisor` passes it and `phase_may_be_live` keeps it, as the plan specifies, so `recover --clean --phase N` exits non-zero until `devflow stop` removes the socket. It used to clean. That sequence was read from the code, not run end to end. Logged for 50.1-25's docs.
- **The 9 s bound is arithmetic on counted waits, not a timing.** The test counts probes and gate-lock acquisitions on paths whose sockets are GONE, then sums the constants. Fsync-bound file operations on a slow disk and a SIGSTOPped `recover` are outside it.
- **The R-07 and R2-07 tests run the interleaving at hook points on one thread.** They prove the fixed order they drive. They do not prove anything about a real concurrent `start`. No test races two processes.
- **The named tests run under nextest pass once each.** One `cargo test --workspace --no-fail-fast` at 61d27e3 and one `scripts/check.sh all` at c0d0f15 were clean. That is a weak bound on flakes in the new retry-based controls.

## Accomplishments

- A stale phase is kept, with the reason and `devflow stop --phase N`, when its recorded socket is ALIVE, STALE or cannot be probed, its pending socket is not GONE or its pending lock is held, a waiter is registered, or its foreground holder is held.
- `clear_state_if` puts the decision, the gate removal, the state removal and the cron removal under one write-lock hold. Every call `recover` makes is bounded by `WRITE_LOCK_REPAIR_WAIT`.
- `Gates::close_phase` closes all five stages under one gate lock, so a decision counts 2 probes and 1 gate wait: 2 x 2 s + 5 s = 9 s, below 10 s.
- `recover --clean` removes abandoned `.live` and `.waiter` files and orphan hold temps with no age bound, and lists every set-aside answer it leaves.
- `lock::{acquire, acquire_blocking, HolderStatus, holder_status, holder_identity, holder, lock_path}` are gone. The checkout lock and the stale sweep stay.
