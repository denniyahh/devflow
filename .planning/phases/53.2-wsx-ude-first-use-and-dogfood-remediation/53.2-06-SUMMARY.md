# Phase 53.2 Plan 06: Fake service manager `active` arm and held-lock readiness Summary

**The fake `systemctl` now reads a unit `active` while its recorded pid lives, and the fake `systemd-run` takes readiness only from the launched phase's runner lock held by the launched pid (read from `/proc/PID/fdinfo`), so e2e tests reach `runner_liveness`'s Stopping-on-active arm and cannot pass on a lock nobody holds (#296, D-14).**

## Accomplishments
- `discard_waits_for_an_active_unit_whose_runner_released_its_lock`: discard, through the binary, waits while the lock is free and the unit reads `active`, removes nothing, and completes once the test's own child is killed. The test first waits for `active` in the fake's `U.active-reads`, so a discard that never asked cannot pass.
- Fake `systemd-run` readiness: it reads the `--phase` value after the command, pads the major part with `printf '%02d'`, and requires a descriptor of the launched pid that is that `.runner-{padded}` (`-ef`) with a `lock:` line in its fdinfo. Each fdinfo read runs in a subshell with stderr discarded, and a vanished fd is skipped.
- Two direct-run negative tests (an unheld `.runner-07` record, and a held `.runner-01` while launched as phase 7). The positive control `start_through_the_fake_launcher_returns_while_the_run_is_live` passes, which confirms RESEARCH assumption A3: the runner lock shows in fdinfo.
