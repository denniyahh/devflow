## Accomplishments
- D-06 (#247): `banner_answer_lines`'s live arm reads the gate's question. A question prints `answer:  devflow gate answer N --gate-id <id> (--option <a|b|c> | --text <answer>)` with ids in offered order. No question prints approve/reject as before. - D-07 (#248): `open_branch_lines` compares a loaded run's branch against its recorded `base_branch`, once per distinct base, and every other branch against the project's base. A base that is not a local branch, or whose branches cannot be listed, prints one could-not-be-compared line instead of a `0 ahead, 0 behind` line.
