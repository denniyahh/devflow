# Phase 53.2 Plan 08: One polling budget and a settled stopped-unit wait Summary

**phase7_cli's seven wait helpers now poll on one CI-aware, overridable budget that each timeout names, both container gates get the 30 s budget, and the #279 stop test waits for `inactive` while reporting any `failed` read with its Result and ExecMainStatus.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** 7 min (05:39:37Z to 05:46:31Z)
- **Tasks:** 3
- **Files modified:** 7

## Accomplishments

- `poll_budget_from(ci, override)`: 10 s; 30 s when `CI` is non-empty and neither `0` nor `false` (case-insensitive); `DEVFLOW_E2E_POLL_BUDGET_SECS` beats it; `ten` panics `DEVFLOW_E2E_POLL_BUDGET_SECS must be whole seconds, got "ten"`, `0` panics `... must be at least 1 second, got "0"`.
- `wait_for`, `wait_for_pid`, `wait_for_state_cleared`, `wait_for_runner_lock_free`, `wait_for_stopped`, `wait_for_settled` and `wait_for_gate` all call `poll_until(poll_budget(), ...)`, which is `test_support::wait_until`. A timeout panics `timed out after {budget:?} waiting for {what}`. No `for _ in 0..` loop remains in the file. `wait_for` and `wait_for_pid` had a 5 s budget before; they now get the shared 10 s.
- `settle_unit_reads(budget, next)` polls every 50 ms: `inactive` returns Ok, a `failed` read returns Err at once with its values, and past the budget it returns Err naming the budget and the last read. `wait_for_unit_inactive(unit, budget)` reads the three properties per poll and panics with `unit {unit} ` before the error.
- `stop_on_a_live_unit_records_stopped` now calls `wait_for_unit_inactive(&guard.unit, STOPPED_UNIT_INACTIVE_WAIT)` (10 s) where it used to read once. The new ignored control `a_unit_that_never_reads_inactive_fails_naming_result_and_exec_main_status` (phase 88) runs the wait on a live unit with a 500 ms budget and asserts that the panic names the unit, `Result=` and `ExecMainStatus=`.
- The devcontainer runCmd exports `DEVFLOW_E2E_POLL_BUDGET_SECS=30` on the line after `set -e`, which stays first. check-in-container.sh passes `-e DEVFLOW_E2E_POLL_BUDGET_SECS="${DEVFLOW_E2E_POLL_BUDGET_SECS:-30}"`, and its header comment explains why `CI` is not forwarded.
