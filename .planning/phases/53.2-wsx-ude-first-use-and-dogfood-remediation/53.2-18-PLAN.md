<objective>
Close the #337 gap: the fake `systemd-run` must wait for the runner's real `READY=1` on a `NOTIFY_SOCKET` listener instead of polling the runner lock in `/proc/PID/fdinfo`. A runner that takes its lock, finishes and exits between two 50 ms polls is then no longer read as a failed start.

Purpose: the verification gap "Stabilize the flaky tests" (53.2-VERIFICATION.md `gaps:`). The 53.2-06 fake added a full-suite flake. The real runner sends `READY=1` only after it holds the launched phase's lock (`pipeline_launch::run_runner`, then `service::notify_ready`). So readiness taken from `READY=1` keeps D-14's intent, no readiness without the held lock, and drops the timing window.
Output: a listener child in `common/mod.rs`, a rewritten readiness arm in the fake, one new direct-run test with its stand-in runner, and the docs that name the change.
Decision note: D-14 said the fake treats "only the launched phase's runner lock, held, as readiness". The operator's #337 gap scope replaces that readiness clause with the real `READY=1`. D-14's `active` clause (fake `systemctl`) is untouched.
Commits: RED `test(53.2-18): add failing test for a runner that exits at once`, GREEN `test(53.2-18): fake systemd-run waits for the runner's READY=1`, docs `test(53.2-18): document the fake's notify listener`, all subjects at most 72 characters.
