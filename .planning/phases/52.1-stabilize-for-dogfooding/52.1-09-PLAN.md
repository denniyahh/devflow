<objective>
Remove `--force` from `devflow start` (D-11, operator decision) and make every place that offered it name `discard` instead; remove the `--no-worktree` stale-base refusal and warning outright (D-24, operator decision, supersedes D-09.4).

Purpose: one `--force` authorised three unrelated destructive acts (recreate a worktree, delete a branch, skip the base check). After this plan none of them is reachable from `start`, and the base check that guarded nothing is gone.
Output: a `start` with no destructive flag and no stale-base check, refusals that name `devflow discard --phase N` on every leftover path, D-10's two start-path refusal tests, the D-24 test, and the doc rows.
</objective>
