<objective>
Remove `--force` from `devflow cleanup` (D-11) and make cleanup remove only what is provably safe (D-09.5): merged, clean, no live run. Everything else is kept with its reason and the verb that removes it.

Purpose: in v4 a finished run ends at Validate and its branch is the only copy of the work until close-out; D-16's proof checks exactly these effects on a real run.
Output: the new cleanup rule (with a per-phase foreground holder, a re-read state, the shared live-run test and the incomplete-run keep), rewritten keep lines, the D-16/D-10 tests, docs and help.
</objective>
