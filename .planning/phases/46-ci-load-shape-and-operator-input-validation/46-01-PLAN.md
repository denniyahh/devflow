in one job, squeezed onto two CPUs, inside the pinned devcontainer image — as an ADDITION alongside
the three existing parallel jobs, not a replacement for them.

shape, but unpinned on the whole runner. The missing ingredient is the CPU pin. CI has rejected 0
of the pushes the local gate rejected 2 of 2. This plan makes the shape EXIST in CI. It does not
claim the job will catch anything.

the local gate and CI, three in-tree guards over the workflow's shape, and a synchronised dev-setup
checklist.

## Assumption delta: promote-vs-add-alongside for the sequential CI job

The deterministic scan fired a `pluralization` signal on the word `alongside` in the ROADMAP
sequencing note. **That lexical match is a false positive** — the sentence is about *when* the job
runs relative to later phases, not about introducing a second X. The checkpoint nonetheless lands
on a real architectural decision, recorded here so it is not re-litigated during execution.

**Noun now primary:** the CI *load shape* (sequential-under-CPU-pressure), not any individual job.

**The fork.** `.github/workflows/devcontainer.yml:52` ALREADY runs `scripts/check.sh all`
sequentially, and its job `Build + test in devcontainer` is ALREADY a required status check on both
`develop` and `main` (verified against the repository rulesets `develop-merge-or-squash` and
`main-squash-only`: 4 required contexts each). The sequential shape is therefore not the gap — the
**2-CPU pin** is (`devcontainer.yml` contains no `taskset` and no `DEVFLOW_CI_CPUS`; both counted 0).

- **PROMOTE** would mean adding the pin to that existing required job.
- **ADD-ALONGSIDE** means creating a new advisory job in `ci.yml` that duplicates the suite run
  under a pin.

**Decision: ADD-ALONGSIDE**, ruled by the operator on 2026-09-04 (D-01, as confirmed). Promoting
would make the most flake-prone configuration a merge blocker on day one, reversing D-05's entire
rationale. Pinning-and-un-requiring was also rejected: it silently drops a sequential-suite merge
guarantee that has gated both branches since July, and this phase's scope fence excludes any change
to branch protection beyond not adding the new job to it.

**Accepted debt:** a duplicated full-suite run. `devcontainer.yml` currently takes ~3m10s
wall-clock, stable across 6/6 recent successful runs, unpinned on 4 vCPU. **The 2-CPU figure is not
measured** — the first real run is the measurement.

**What would force a later promote:** the advisory job earning promotion across this milestone
(D-05). Promotion then means folding the pin into `devcontainer.yml`'s required job and deleting the
advisory one. **Not this phase.**
</assumption_delta_decision>
